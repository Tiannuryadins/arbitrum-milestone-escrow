Freelance escrow, without the middleman.

Clients lock payment in a smart contract before work starts. Freelancers submit proof of work per milestone. Payment releases on approval — or a neutral arbitrator decides a dispute. No platform fees, no frozen payouts, no trust required. Built for the Arbitrum Open House Dubai Buildathon.

  1. 1. Fund — client creates an escrow and locks ETH.
  2. 2. Milestones — work is split into priced milestones.
  3. 3. Submit — freelancer attaches proof of work.
  4. 4. Release — client approves, ETH pays out instantly.
  5. 5. Dispute — arbitrator resolves if things go wrong.

How the demo works

  • Use two wallets (or two browser profiles): one as client, one as freelancer.
  • Client creates an escrow, adds milestones, then the freelancer submits work with a proof link (IPFS CID or URL).
  • Client approves → ETH is released on-chain. Or either side disputes → the arbitrator resolves.
  • Everything is on Arbitrum Sepolia testnet — get free testnet ETH from a Sepolia faucet and bridge it via bridge.arbitrum.io.
